TEAMS - SUGGESTED WORKFLOW
=========================

01 Check Teams App and Connection
  Check New Teams package, process and basic endpoint connectivity.
02 Restart or Clear Teams Cache
  Try Restart first. Choose ClearCache only if still needed.
  Teams closes; active calls are interrupted. Cache is moved to a backup.

Then reopen Teams and verify sign-in, messaging and a test call.
If audio or camera still fails, use 11 Audio Camera and Devices.
TCP reachability is not proof that a tenant service or sign-in works.
Run in the affected user's session. Classic Teams is not covered.
