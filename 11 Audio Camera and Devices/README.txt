AUDIO, CAMERA AND DEVICES
=========================

  Order: 01 check -> 02 targeted restart or Settings -> practical test.

TOOLS
-----
  01  Check Audio Camera and Dock Devices
      - Connected devices, errors, audio defaults, services and consent.
      - Check the device chosen inside the affected app as well.
  02  Restart Selected Device or Audio
      - Restart the audio service or one selected audio/camera device.
      - OpenSettings helps with manual device choice or permission
        inspection.

NOTES
-----
  - Test actual playback, microphone recording, camera preview and dock
    ports.
  - No driver removal or automatic privacy-setting changes are performed.
  - USB hubs, docks, storage, display, input and network devices are not
    restarted automatically because they can affect unrelated work and
    connectivity.
