AUDIO, CAMERA AND DEVICES - SUGGESTED WORKFLOW
=============================================

01 Check Audio Camera and Dock Devices
  Inspect connected devices, errors, audio defaults, services and consent.
  Check the device chosen inside the affected app as well.
02 Restart Selected Device or Audio
  Restart audio service or one selected audio/camera device if appropriate.
  OpenSettings helps with manual device choice or permission inspection.

Test actual playback, microphone recording, camera preview and dock ports.
No driver removal or automatic privacy-setting changes are performed.
USB hubs, docks, storage, display, input and network devices are not restarted
automatically because they can affect unrelated work and connectivity.
