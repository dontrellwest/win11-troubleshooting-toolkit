NEW OUTLOOK
===========

  Order: 01 check first; 02 reset only when the checks point at local data.

TOOLS
-----
  01  Check New Outlook App and Service
      - Open folder 01 and run its CMD in the affected user's session.
      - Read Verdict, NextStep, WebView2, service and local-data findings.
      - Compare missing messages with Outlook on the web.
      - Resolve filters, Focused/Other, connectivity or service issues first.
  02  Reset New Outlook Mail Cache
      - Consider when the local app/cache still appears broken after those
        checks.
      - Confirm important mail and drafts are on the server.
      - Read the repair guide and preview before moving local data aside.

NOTES
-----
  - Afterward, sign in if prompted and rerun check 01.
  - The diagnostic reports local signals; it cannot prove mailbox
    synchronization.
